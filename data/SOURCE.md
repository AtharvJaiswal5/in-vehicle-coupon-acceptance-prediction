# Dataset attribution

In-Vehicle Coupon Recommendation [Dataset]. (2017). UCI Machine Learning Repository. https://doi.org/10.24432/C5GS4P

Downloaded 1 October 2026 from https://archive.ics.uci.edu/static/public/603/in%2Bvehicle%2Bcoupon%2Brecommendation.zip . The original CSV is supplied unchanged. License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ . The license permits redistribution with attribution.

The records describe survey responses collected through Amazon Mechanical Turk. Acceptance is stated intention within hypothetical driving scenarios, not observed redemption. See Wang et al. (2017), https://jmlr.org/papers/v18/16-003.html .

The source CSV uses `passanger` and `has_children`; these exact names are preserved. Original row numbers in the split manifest are zero-based data-row positions, excluding the CSV header.
